Privacy Policy

Last updated October 25, 2026

Jarvis Social Desk is a private tool used only by its owner, Osvaldo Guajardo.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the Google Drive API to upload, list, move and delete video files in the owner's own Drive folders.

How data is used

Files are used only to prepare and publish the owner's social media posts. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. The app does not collect data from anyone else.

Storage and removal

Files stay in the owner's Google Drive. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
